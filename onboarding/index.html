06:00 UTC and 18:00 UTC.

Asia is awake for one. The Americas for the other. Almost no one is locked out of both.

Pick 10 or 20 minutes before you start.

One anchor: the breath, or the feet. When attention leaves, come back. Stopping on time is the practice.
